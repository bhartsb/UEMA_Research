Original synergy review and methods.

The synergy planner began its search from a zero intention, which its decoder defines as holding the current pose. Predictions that ignore the order provide no useful direction for that search. Changing the smoother at the same time also slowed the body's response. Both changes need separate tests.

What changed

  • The movement map can now learn while its planner and exploration remain off. The bootstrap retains proposal smoothing. Giving the map control requires a paired physical comparison.
  • When enabled, the synergy planner starts from the encoded controller proposal. This is still a projection through the learned map; a fresh map is not assumed ready to control.
  • Following now counts completed executed orders by author, with requested and realized turns and a random-axis geometric control. Stillness receives no following credit. This control is not a physical replay.
  • The five unfinished model lanes were integrated. Merge interactions in controllability fields, vestibular loss weighting, resume settings and copied probe state were repaired.
  • Non-finite synergy exploration falls back to independent exploration. Non-finite gradients in added modules now reach the existing optimizer skip guard.
  • A real-checkpoint test exposed a one-window liveness repair: units active only during startup escaped repair forever. The repair now keeps observing unrepaired units and never resets a previously repaired unit.
  • Dashboard, explainer and watcher readers were brought into line with the integrated producers. The movement map's reconstruction is no longer counted in the shared-model loss-share denominator.

Recorded qualification

  • Eight bodies trained for 400 model steps on a private copy: passed. Operational checks.
  • All nine repaired layers were observed at least 100 training steps after their first repair. The latest readings had 79–100 responding units per 100, above the required 50. Liveness evidence.
  • Focused suites and browser checks are recorded in the detailed review. The original checkpoint and recording tapes were preserved.

What would establish faster, better gain of function

The model must predict the consequences of its orders, use those predictions to choose effective actions, and repeat, retain and transfer the resulting abilities. More motion, a lower loss or responsive units alone does not demonstrate that chain.

The next comparison must train the upgrade and the previous configuration with matched experience and wall-clock budgets. Before either synergy control switch is enabled, test each switch separately from the same checkpoint and world starts, with uncertainty across whole orders and bodies.

No result in this takeover yet establishes faster acquisition, retention or transfer. Imagination loss remains outside the next-frame normalization change; its contribution and representation quality still need measurement during learning. The learning-progress score can also move when world conditions change with frozen weights; without a same-moment control, that score cannot establish learning.
