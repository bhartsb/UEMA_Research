A hit is a number a reader cannot place. There are four kinds:
  - a score below 1 with no unit, such as 'direction match 0.14' (the rule the pages already had);
  - a score of 1 or more with no unit, such as 'error ratio 1.003' (new today);
  - a multiplier with nothing named after it, such as 'drift x1.0' (new today;
    '4.2 times its first value' passes);
  - a raw input value of a hidden unit, such as 'their inputs ranged from -32.9 to -7.1' (new today).
A number passes when it is a count ('32 of every 100'), carries a real unit (cm, degrees, s, GB,
steps, per second), is a percentage, or names the end of a scale ('1.0 = one millimetre').
