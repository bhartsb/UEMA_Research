Twenty 2026 papers read against UEMA v3.5

Reading notes written November 5, 2026. Each page explains, in plain terms, what the paper did, what it measured, where it touches the UEMA specification (docs/UEMA_Specs/UEMA_Spec_v3_5.txt), how it could help, and what to be careful about. Section numbers are v3.5's. Every paper was read in full from arXiv; numbers are the authors' own.

How the papers were chosen

The search covered the themes the specification is built from: latent forward models and imagination drift, planning and the §10.6 detour problem, encoder collapse, tactile and gaze sensing, wellness and interoception, curiosity, emotion, the MIMo body, demonstrations, and hierarchies of minds. Papers were kept if they were from 2026, if their full text could be read, and if they bore on a named section of the specification or on the fall-and-recovery, reach-and-grasp and demonstration-replay work of October and November 2026. Three are theory or review papers with no experiments and are marked as such. Four are on the MIMo body itself.

The five that seem most immediately actionable, in the reader's judgement: the kinematic-rollout diagnostic (a cheap test of the forward model's physics), the hierarchical planning paper (the detour problem with a control experiment), the infant motion retargeting pipeline (Stream 3's missing front end, released, on MIMo), Curiosity-Critic (a principled replacement for the curiosity band's upper edge), and UWM-JEPA's training lesson that the next-step loss can leave the action input inert.
