N1. (Wording I suggested in report.txt.) "...and 6 of the 8 ended nearer the sent position than T: most hands went
    toward the position they were sent, not toward T."
    Fails: ending nearer the sent position than T compares two distances at the end; it does not show the direction
    of motion. Body 0's left hand started 3.14 cm from its sent position and ended 3.94 cm from it, farther, yet
    "nearer the sent position than T" (hands16 rows, on run: L 3.14, a_r 3.94, a_T 17.78). "not toward T" also
    contradicts the page's own "only 5 of the 8 ended nearer than they started".
    Fact: all 8 hands moved along the line from their start toward the sent position, 0.23 cm to 5.43 cm (hands16
    rows, on run, a_along: 1.54, 3.11, 0.23, 4.2, 5.43, 4.99, 4.05, 5.21).
    Fix: "...and 6 of the 8 ended nearer the sent position than T. All 8 moved along the line from their start toward
    the sent position (0.23 cm to 5.43 cm), and 5 of the 8 also ended nearer T than they started."

N2. "Success: each of the 8 hands of bodies 0, 1, 5 and 6 ends nearer T at 250 ms than in the on run by more than
    0.00003 cm, no fall flag rises, and none of the 4 bodies' measured pose-error score rises above the on run's."
    Fails: 0.00003 cm is how closely the physics repeats an identical command. A difference beyond it shows that the
    command made the difference, not that the difference is large enough to matter. As written, every hand improving
    by 0.001 cm would be called "Success", against section 5's "the size of improvements and of setbacks matters" and
    the rule quoted in step 1 ("no ... promotion from a marginal difference"). Success also says nothing about
    reaching: every hand could improve while none comes within 2.0 cm.
    Fix: say what the line shows and what it does not: "Success: each of the 8 hands ... ends nearer T than in the on
    run by more than 0.00003 cm (the repeat spread: a difference beyond it comes from the command, whatever its size)
    ...; the change in cm for each hand and the median are reported beside it, and success here is neither reaching
    (the 2.0 cm line) nor adoption." If the overseer wants a size line, it is set before the run, with its reason.
