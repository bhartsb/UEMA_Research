What this means for the goal

The repaired motor connection removes an avoidable obstacle; it is not evidence that intention emerged. The model still needs to learn which coordinated body changes improve its own experienced state, then use that prediction to choose and correct a movement.

  1. Measure an actual side turn and recovery. Use the corrected roll measure together with realized contact and body-function observations. Compare identified frozen checkpoints in matched worlds. Count spontaneous motion separately from returning to a selected state after an intervention.
  2. Recheck action consequences under the repaired decoder. Earlier body-forecast results remain dated experiments under the earlier mapping. They do not validate forecasts for this repaired body.
  3. Earn adoption of the self-state perception separately. Preserve the old model in a copy, test learning and recovery under reversible functional disturbance, and require retained abilities and better realized outcomes before allowing its new chooser to control live behavior. The earlier implementation's neutral preservation checks do not establish this gain.

Two eye torsion actuators and four toe actuators still have no independent handles. The five repaired trunk actuators are not among those omissions. This review does not establish that those omissions prevent rolling, or that the five trunk commands alone guarantee a complete roll.
