A functioning self that wants to discover what it can do.

Wellness is a perception of the self: its body, usable senses, reserves, capabilities and learned relations. Intention should change that perceived future, including gaining reliable capabilities while the body is already comfortable.

This plan replaces the deficit-only interpretation. It specifies mechanisms, implementation boundaries, failure tests and the minimum environments for every ability. It does not equate working software with a developed being.

1. What is wrong, and what direction is credible?

The earlier architecture could train predictions for days without learning a reason to roll. A low average prediction error can describe a mostly unchanged scene. A scalar comfort objective can prefer a stationary body. Adding a caregiver does not, by itself, connect controllable discoveries to the self's future. Increasing memory slots does not create those discoveries.

The credible route is a closed loop: experience actual bodily consequences; distinguish what action controls; predict the alternatives on the timescale the body responds; preserve functioning; and pursue demonstrated opportunities to learn useful distinctions. Here, useful means extending or maintaining the self's functioning and access, not pleasing an external task scorer.

Engineering judgment: this architecture can support the proposed early motor functions if its mechanics allow them and the new learning loop produces enough controlled experience. Current evidence does not justify assuming all four stages will emerge from unattended prediction training. We now test physical possibility, information availability, motivation, action selection and retained behavior separately so failure has an actionable location.

The initial MIMo brain is roughly 5.5 million parameters; the added grounded/spatial self model brings this configuration to roughly 5.73 million. This is a body-learning test bed, not the planned 280-million-parameter UE brain. Parameter count is neither an ability measurement nor a substitute for bodily experience. Measure simulated seconds, completed action consequences and retained control per unit of compute.

2. The complete perception–intention loop

Physical contacts first produce intrinsic signals: local pressure, vibration, sliding, force and joint state. The body's modulation operator changes available sensing or capacity. Encoders preserve local identity and spatial relations. The self contains both observed bodily functioning and learned beliefs about consequences; an observed function channel is not itself learned self-recognition.

The predictor receives recent motion and the actual held, smoothed command sequence plus elapsed time. A hypothetical command is not an achieved pose. Future self state is predicted as a change from current grounded state. A frozen acting copy supplies stable perception and choices while the learner updates from actual experience. A promoted replacement keeps its own continuous history and the latest learner's earned weights.

When functioning is threatened

Compare alternatives using local availability, capacity and uncertainty. Prefer a feasible future that restores functioning. A temporary effort cost can be acceptable; a fixed pose or zero movement is not the homeostatic target. Distinguish injury proxies, sensor availability and muscle fatigue instead of averaging them into one number.

When the body is already well

Use completed evidence of improving, action-dependent prediction to identify where another action could acquire reliable distinctions. Compare against persistence, held commands and shuffled commands. Keep a historical best so forgetting and relearning do not earn repeated credit. Mastered repetition satiates. Uncontrolled noise earns no opportunity.

A positive opportunity is a processing-state component of the perceived self. It is not a label saying “roll now.” Existing unlabelled motor exploration provides bootstrap experience when the new audit has no evidence. Its contribution and any scheduled overrides remain visible. The acquisition route must beat a matched comparison in actual learned control while maintaining viable function before replacing the checked controller.

The pressure and pleasure interpretation

Force is measured in newtons; pressure is force divided by a physically justified area. A sharp edge can concentrate stress at constant total force. In MIMo, dividing 7.49 N by 97.92 cm² gives about 765 Pa; that measured adapter result does not establish the true tissue footprint of a small sphere. We must not call it a biologically calibrated pain reading. Toddler pain thresholds cannot be derived from impact force alone: stopping distance, contact shape, tissue, location and duration matter.

The resolution variable q is an operator index, not “percent of information remaining.” At q=1 the current operator preserves the original signal. Lower values select a coarse quantization grid. For a 100 kPa range, nine bits give roughly 196 Pa spacing; twelve bits roughly 24 Pa. Whether that loses a relevant distinction depends on the pressure contrast being sensed. Therefore a generic q > 0.8 cannot certify intact perception. Calibrate actual distinguishability at each relevant site and keep muscle-capacity criteria separate.

Small positive modulation should have headroom or improve another real processing capacity; clipping every healthy sensor at its maximum makes that route inert. Healthy development must not require injuring the body first. The present controlled-learning opportunity supplies an additional processing-state route, while positive sensory modulation remains explicitly measurable rather than assumed.

6. Execution order, tests and deployment

Current deployment: grounded spatial self learning and local skin modulation are live. A separate frozen chooser observes the same world while the existing learner continues to control the body. The migration preserved every earlier tensor, optimizer history and physical episode. This is a working integration, not a passed developmental test. Read the current verification and remaining work; machine-readable receipt.

Learning-audit limitation found in the live path: the existing observer grades updated predictor weights using its older frozen sensory coordinates. That measures compatibility with the old encoder, not the continuing learner's complete ability. Its 50-millisecond outcome also samples only the beginning of each 250-millisecond held command. A zero reading cannot establish absence of learning, and a positive reading cannot qualify the healthy-exploration controller. Preserve these records as diagnostics. A replacement must train and evaluate compatible representations, retain fixed targets, and test outcomes over actual executed command sequences. The observer remains unqualified while this is resolved.

  1. Preserve continuity and complete saves. Validate weights, optimizer moments, current perception, physical world, action smoother, RNG, history and curriculum. Put large checkpoint files on monitored storage. Recover from the last complete save after a hardware/storage fault; record the exact unsaved interval.
  2. Activate real function experience and continued grounded learning. Migrate every old parameter and optimizer state unchanged. Import only the added grounded module's earned evidence from a copied study. Newly added spatial weights start neutral. Do not replace the latest old brain with an earlier copied-study checkpoint.
  3. Measure physical opportunity and actual behavior immediately. Run target relocation, removal, interruption and settle tests; run body-only floor movement and rolling reachability; record action authorship, contacts, orientation, simulated exposure and full replay receipts.
  4. Qualify action-sensitive self forecasts and healthy acquisition. Separate skin and motor domains; the useful motor forecast is not evidence of a working touch forecast. Check prediction error against the physical separation needed to choose. Acquisition may qualify on newly controlled distinctions or physical task success, with retained functioning and retention.
  5. Compare interventions by learned function. Keep the original caregiver-versus-replay study; add the scheduled-stillness comparison with equal physical experience and equal exploration schedule. No result based only on lower prediction error or greater movement can decide these studies.
  6. Run subsequent stages when physical prerequisites exist. Free-object manipulation, floor mobility, support-assisted transitions and tool retrieval use the same evidence contract. An unavailable measurement is reported as unassessable, never zero. Network self-recognition uses representation tests, not a renamed body coordinate.
  7. Promote only a qualified acting candidate. Stage it on the actual stream; preserve candidate history through promotion; continue learner updates. A failed test leaves the old controller operating and produces a named repair task. No endless waiting for another permission question is built into routine testing.

Every queue job records its checkpoint, declaration, exact command, output directory, process identity and result hash. Stopped measurements retry in a new directory with bounded retries. Experiment timing is estimated from completed work when available; wall-clock promises are not fabricated. Experiment failure must not stop unrelated production learning.

Acceptance is concrete: all old trained state survives migration; actual next-frame modulation is observed; spatial information reaches self forecasts; actor and learner continue independently; noise/forgetting controls do not create false opportunity; the recorder measures real rolling rather than yaw; missing/empty/NaN evidence cannot pass; completed behavior results appear in the dashboard and history; the old dashboard still updates.

7. Transfer to UE Avatar and remaining scientific limits

Transfer interfaces should carry units, stable intrinsic site identity, local position/normal, actual elapsed time, available resolution, regional response and executed actuator history. UE's denser fingertips should preserve their additional distinctions and regional sensitivity. Do not collapse them to MIMo's pooled summary or interpret sensor count as proportional pleasure. Recalibrate contact footprint, force limits and action cadence on UE; MIMo thresholds and handle indices are not automatically transferable.

Keep old motor/perception weights and add neutral adapters where layouts differ. Test identical physical meaning across both bodies, then measure retention and transfer of learned relations. More parameters and richer sensing may increase capacity, but success depends on adequate information, controllability and experience. The avatar's own vocal tract remains deferred by the user's instruction; caregiver speech is already a separate environmental channel.

Expanded self is a functional hypothesis: an object, person or place becomes represented in relation to continued capability and regulation. Causal dependence is not ownership; ownership is not consciousness. Tool use must survive changed tools and goals. Loss should provoke context-appropriate regulation, not a hard-coded replacement purchase. No complete empirical equation of consciousness exists here; we can implement and falsify the observable developmental mechanisms without claiming that software integration has created subjective experience.
