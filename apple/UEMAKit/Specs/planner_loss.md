The ring is a `deque(maxlen=200)` of `(tokens {sense: [B, 192]}, wball [B])` appended every model step (lem_model.py:998, :4271-4272) beside the channel record `_goal_ring_ch` (:1012, :4280).
- `wellness_rank normalized` (args.json:180): the ring index per body is `best_t = argmax over T of ranked[T, B]` where `ranked` comes from `_normalized_goal_values` (lem_model.py:8109-8128) -> `rank_values` (wellness_channels.py:488-533): `contrib[t, c] = w_c (v_tc - centre_c) / max(scale_c, floor_c)` for each ranked channel with weight w_c != 0 and a finite value (unmeasured -> 0); `value = sum_c contrib`; ring entries (model steps) whose `harm` row exceeds the gate line 0.92 (`INTEGRITY_GATE_LINE`, :206) get `-inf`, unless every entry of a body is over it, then its least harmful entries keep their value. Weights: the receipt's `wellness_channels.weights` (integrity 2.0, controllability 1.0, learning_progress 0.75, coherence 0.5, contact_control 2.0, consequence 0.5, self_value 0.5, agency 0.25, prediction_error 0.25, tactile_comfort 0.25, capability 0.25, movement 0.25, curiosity 0.25, efficiency 0.0), controllability set to 0 while its evidence gate is shut (`_rank_weights_now`, lem_model.py:8099-8106); centre and scale: the channel normaliser's running mean and spread (wellness_channels.py:433-439; horizon 2000 model steps, :131); floors: the receipt's `wellness_channels.floors` (= `FLOORS`, wellness_channels.py:149-180). The stored values are each channel's distance from the body's running average over 10 model steps (`detrend_moment`, :449-485; `RING_DETREND_STEPS` 10, :382; receipt `detrend_steps` 10); pain and harm are stored raw. How each channel value is made is in wellness_sum.md section 4.
- `goal_locality off` (args.json:88): `goal_state[m] = ring tokens[best_t[b]][m]` per body (lem_model.py:4314-4317).
- `current_state` = this model step's raw tokens (:4303); `current_motor` = the motor controller's output of this model step (`motor_out`, :3107, handed to `_plan_chain` at :4358-4359); `max_steps` = 1 (`hand_target_mode wrist_relative_v1`, lem_model.py:6218-6219, args.json:187; `plan_horizon earned` may license more, lem_model.py:4327-4334, and is overridden to 1).
